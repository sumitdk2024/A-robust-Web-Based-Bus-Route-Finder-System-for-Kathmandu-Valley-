INTRODUCTION
I am developing a web-based bus route finder system for Kathmandu Valley. As public transport has always been a source of confusion to me personally and from what I have observed most people are also struggling with it. Locating the right bus, understanding where it is going, and figuring out which route to take is a challenging task, especially for new users or visitors who are unfamiliar with the area. Most of the time people depend on asking others or guessing the routes and this leads to wasting time and condition of frustration.


Through this project, a simple web platform will be developed where users can provide their point of origin and destination and the system will come up with the most appropriate bus routes. It might seem straightforward but the steps to be taken include handling the route data, creating a user-friendly interface, and embedding the algorithms to figure out the optimal paths.In the end, I will have not only a working system but also documentation explaining how such a system could be implemented in real-world transportation systems.

PROJECT BACKGROUND
Currently, public transport is one of the major modes of travel in Kathmandu Valley but it does not have proper digital support yet.
Current Problems in Kathmandu Public Transport:
Passengers often depend on conductors or other passengers to know where buses go. This makes it difficult for new users to travel confidently.
Bus stops are not clearly defined or digitally mapped. Many buses stop at informal locations, which adds to confusion.
There is no system to plan routes in advance. People cannot check which bus to take before leaving home, leading to wasted time and inefficient travel.
Tourists and people from outside the valley struggle the most, as they are unfamiliar with routes and language barriers make it harder.

Why This Matters:

People spend unnecessary time figuring out routes. Students and workers may be late because they take incorrect buses. Public transport becomes less attractive compared to private vehicles, increasing traffic congestion.
This is not just inconvenient—it affects productivity and daily life. A simple digital solution could make a big difference.

Why Now:

With increasing internet access and smartphone usage in Nepal, a web-based system is practical and useful. Digital mapping tools are available, making it easier to build such systems. This project can demonstrate how technology can improve public transportation.



AIMS
The main aim is to develop a functional web-based bus route finder system that helps users easily navigate public transportation in Kathmandu Valley. I also aim to understand how

route optimization works and how user-friendly systems can improve daily commuting. By the end, I will have a working prototype and documentation showing how such a system could be implemented on a larger scale.
